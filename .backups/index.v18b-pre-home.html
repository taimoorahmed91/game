Neon
Multiplier

Lead your squad down the road. Pick the best gate to grow it, shoot the aliens, and beat the boss at the end of each level.

  • Green and blue gates add soldiers or speed up your fire
  • Red gates and aliens cost you soldiers
  • Shoot crates to turn their number positive, then drive into them
  • Move forward or back to line up: forward means less time to react
  • Press Space to jump over holes in the road
