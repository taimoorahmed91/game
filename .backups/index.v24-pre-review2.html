How to play

  • Steer: ← → or swipe: lanes. ↑ ↓ or drag: forward / back (forward = less time).
  • Jump: Space, tap or JUMP to leap over holes. Enemies and boss slams still hit you in the air: step out of the way.
  • Gates: green and blue help, red hurt. The middle lane skips them.
  • Crates: shoot to raise the number, drive in once it is positive.
  • Fire gates are temporary: they last 15 seconds.
  • Aliens carry a golden core: kill 3 to charge BOOST.
  • BOOST: double-tap, double-click or tap Ctrl for 6s of super power.
  • Every level starts with 1 soldier. Beat the boss to finish.
  • Keys: Esc or P pause · M sound · H home
